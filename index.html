PersonalOS

A private, single-user personal operations system.

PersonalOS is software its owner runs for their own household administration. It is not a public service and does not accept users or sign-ups.

With the owner's explicit consent, it reads the owner's own Gmail mailbox read-only to keep an auditable record of incoming communications. It never sends, deletes, labels, or otherwise changes email.
