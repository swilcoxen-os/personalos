PersonalOS Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · PersonalOS

Who uses PersonalOS

PersonalOS is a private application operated by a single individual for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's data is intentionally collected.

Google user data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, PersonalOS uses the read-only Gmail scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) on the owner's own mailbox. It reads the mailbox profile and its change history to observe which messages arrived.

How the data is used

  • To keep an auditable record of incoming communications for the owner's personal administration.
  • Message content may be read transiently to verify operation. PersonalOS stores message and thread identifiers, timestamps, and non-content metadata and digests, not message bodies.
  • PersonalOS never sends, deletes, labels, or modifies email.

Storage and security

Data is stored only in the owner's private database and private hosting. OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest and are available only to the owner's own service.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. PersonalOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls all stored data and may delete it at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
